# ZippyGo SEO setup

Implemented:
- Search-friendly titles and meta descriptions for airport transfers, car hire and airport parking.
- Canonical URLs.
- robots.txt and XML sitemap.
- WebSite, Organization, Service and FAQ structured data.
- Dedicated crawlable landing pages at `/airport-transfers/`, `/car-hire/` and `/airport-parking/`.
- Internal links between the three service pages.
- Dynamic React SEO metadata for the application.

## Important: ranking first
No code can guarantee that ZippyGo appears first in Google/Bing. Rankings depend on relevance, authority, competition, location, backlinks, technical quality and search engine policies.

## Required after deployment
1. Verify `https://zippygotransfers.com/` in Google Search Console.
2. Submit `https://zippygotransfers.com/sitemap.xml`.
3. Verify the same property in Bing Webmaster Tools and submit the sitemap.
4. Build genuine destination/service content and reputable backlinks.
5. Keep business information, contact details and legal pages consistent across the site.
6. Do not create doorway pages or keyword-stuffed airport pages without useful unique content.
